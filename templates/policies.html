Privacy & location terms

Your account

Your email and optional profile address are visible only in your own profile. Public reviews show your username. Clearing your address and saving removes it. Deleting your account removes your profile, sessions, ratings and reviews; submitted business information remains for moderation.

Location services

When Google location services are configured, address text is sent to Google for suggestions and location lookup. Suggestions start after three characters. Device location is requested only when you select “Use my current location”; coordinates are sent to this app to calculate distances. Search locations are not saved and are sent in request bodies rather than page URLs. A saved profile address is used only when you explicitly select it.

Google Maps features are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Google business names, addresses and attribution are retrieved live. User-entered listings and community fry ratings are separate from Google content.

Business requests

Business request details are visible to the submitter and administrators. Approved business names, addresses and descriptions become public. Submit business information you are authorized to share.
